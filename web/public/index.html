Notifications that work your way.

Small, scriptable cards that drip from your Mac’s notch. Connect them to your tools and control what deserves your attention.

brew install jrnxf/tap/drip

Running in a minute.

A prebuilt binary for Apple silicon and Intel. No Xcode.

  1. Install

    brew install jrnxf/tap/drip

  2. Send a card

    No permission prompt. drip draws its own card, not a Notification Center banner.

    drip ok "hello"

  3. Pick a recipe

    Snippets to copy and adapt: slow shell commands, coding-agent permission cards, or all recipes.

Small, and it stays out of the way.

macOS 13 or newer. A notch is optional: without one, cards come from the top edge. Cards wait while the screen is locked.

texta title of up to 2 lines and a detail of up to 3. Pipe text in and the first line is the title
time4.5 s by default. --seconds takes 0.5 to 60. --stay keeps it until clicked
buttons--open opens a URL or a file. --yes / --no ask, then exit 0 or 1
agentsClaude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, through a recipe's hook
